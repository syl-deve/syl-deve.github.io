HOW TO PLAY

Three steps is all it takes

New players get the hang of it in one match.

  1. Aim

    Drag the barrel to set your angle. Always check the wind arrow.

  2. Charge

    Hold the fire button to build power.

  3. Fire

    Release at the right moment to launch your shell.

Land three direct hits first or have more hits when 20 turns end to win.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. There are in-app gold purchases and optional rewarded ads. Gold is for cosmetics and never affects who wins.

What if no one is online?

If Quick Match finds no rival within 15 seconds you play a practice match against an AI gunner. AI gunners show (AI) next to their names and never count toward rank. Waiting players are always matched with each other first.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. In Invite a Friend tap Create Invite Room to get a 6-character code. The duel starts as soon as your friend enters the same code.

Can I play solo?

Yes. AI battles come in Easy, Normal and Hard and you can play them any time. The Daily Challenge lets you shoot on your own and compete on a daily leaderboard without waiting for a rival.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Every day all gunners play the same terrain with the same wind order. The goal is to hit the enemy castle three times within 6 shots. More hits rank higher, then fewer shots, then a faster time. You get 3 ranked attempts a day and practice runs never count. The ranking resets at midnight Korea time.

Is it on iPhone?

Android only for now.

Which languages are supported?

English and Korean.
